Frequently asked

How do I play?

Hold anywhere on the screen to lock your ship into orbit around the nearest planet. Let go to fly straight along your heading. Reach the exit gate to clear the level.

How do I get 3 stars?

Collect all three stardust shards in a single flight, then reach the gate.

What do the new hazards do?

Each new mechanic is introduced with a short card the first time you meet it. Unstable stars collapse when their ring runs out, pulse gates only let you through while open, wormholes carry you to their far mouth, and comets and solar flares sweep across your path.

What is the Constellation Journal?

Every day there is one daily orbit, the same for every player. Each one you clear lights a star in this month's constellation and grows your streak. If you miss a day, a streak saver covers it automatically.

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

Your progress is saved on your device. On Android it is included in your Google backup; on iPhone it is included in your iCloud or device backup. Restore the backup on your new phone to keep your levels and streak.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open Settings in the game (the gear on the home screen) and tap Privacy. This option appears in regions where consent is required, such as the EU and UK. You can also reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

I bought Remove ads. How do I restore it?

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore, signed in with the same Google or Apple account you used to buy it. A reinstall usually restores it on its own.

How do I ask you to delete my data?

Email us and we will delete the analytics data linked to your device. See the privacy policy for details.
