Privacy Policy

Orbit Drift · Effective October 22, 2026

Who we are

Orbit Drift is a space arcade game made by Hrvoje Bencik ("we"). Contact: hrvojebencik1@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, daily streak, settings) is saved only on your device. You do not create an account, and we never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

What is collected

  • Gameplay analytics (GameAnalytics). Events such as level started, won or lost, ads shown, and streak length, with a random device id, device model, operating system, app version and the country derived from your IP address. We use this to balance levels and fix problems.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob). To show ads, Google receives your device's advertising identifier, IP address and ad interactions. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy choices. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

We do not sell your data. Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS).

Purchases

If you buy Remove ads, the payment is handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details; the store only tells the game that the purchase exists.

Third parties

  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy
  • GameAnalytics: gameanalytics.com/privacy

Your choices

  • Change ad consent in Settings › Privacy choices (where offered).
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your phone's settings.
  • Ask us to delete analytics data linked to your device id by emailing us.

Children

Orbit Drift is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
