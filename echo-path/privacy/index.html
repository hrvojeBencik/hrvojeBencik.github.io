Privacy Policy

Who we are

Echo Path is a maze game made by Hrvoje Bencik ("we"). Contact: hrvojebencik1@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, daily streak, goals and settings) is saved only on your device. You do not create an account, and we never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

Gameplay analytics

We use GameAnalytics to balance levels and fix problems. It receives gameplay events (level started, won or lost, ads shown, purchases) with a random device id that is not your advertising ID, plus device model, operating system, app version and the country derived from your IP address. It starts only after the ad consent step.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google receives your device's advertising identifier, IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), basic device information and your interactions with ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy choices. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

If you buy Remove ads, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your payment details.

We do not sell your data. Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS).

Third parties

  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from apps
  • GameAnalytics: gameanalytics.com/privacy

Your choices

  • Change ad consent in Settings › Privacy choices (where offered).
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your phone's settings.
  • Delete all game data by uninstalling the app. To delete analytics data linked to your device id, or for any other data request, email us.

Children

Echo Path is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
